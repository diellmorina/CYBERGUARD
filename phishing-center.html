Phishing Red Flags

🚨 Common Warning Signs:

  • Urgent language ("Act now!", "Verify immediately")
  • Requests for passwords or personal information
  • Misspelled domain names or suspicious sender addresses
  • Unusual or unexpected attachments
  • Suspicious links (hover to see actual URL)
  • Poor grammar or spelling
  • Generic greetings ("Dear Customer")
  • Threats of account closure or legal action

✅ Safe Practices:

  • Never click links in unsolicited emails
  • Verify sender's identity before responding
  • Go to websites directly instead of clicking email links
  • Contact organizations through official phone numbers
  • Check for HTTPS and official website domains
  • Be suspicious of unexpected attachments
  • Report phishing to the organization
  • Keep software updated with security patches
